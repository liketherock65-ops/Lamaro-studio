The story

More than a shop.
A platform for women.

Lamaro Studio's public profile describes a social-impact business built to help women entrepreneurs and designers showcase products, connect, learn and reach markets.

From Gulu outward

Local talent deserves visibility.

Lamaro Studio is a Gulu-based creative studio centred on women-led creativity, handmade work and fashion. Its Women’s Day Fashion Show is part of that public-facing creative presence.

The website makes that mission part of the customer experience — not an afterthought.

Women in Business

Connection creates opportunity.

Lamaro's archived public site describes monthly Women in Business meetings focused on discussion, networking, personal development and coaching. That community layer is part of what makes the brand different from a normal gift shop.
